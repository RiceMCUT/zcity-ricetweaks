`lua/zcity_rice_tweaks/modifications/weapons/cl_optic_scope.lua` replaces z-city `homigrad_base` SWEP:DoRT with a lens-mesh-UV based render.RenderView scope (EFT-style). Cvars: zc_rice_scope_enabled, zc_rice_scope_rtsize (256-2048 square, live), zc_rice_scope_shadow, zc_rice_scope_cheap (copies main frame at PostDrawTranslucentRenderables via render.CopyRenderTargetToTexture and magnifies it instead of a 2nd RenderView; hides own weapon by wrapping hg.RenderWeapons and setting RENDERING_SCOPE, which z-city DrawWorldModel checks), zc_rice_scope_debug (fills ZCityRiceTweaks.ScopeDebug). Missing-material (`___error`) glass meshes on sight models are hidden via SetSubMaterial(slot, 'null'). The 'outside-scope low-res' RenderScene feature was tried and removed (low benefit).
